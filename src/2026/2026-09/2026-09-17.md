Eosinophils.

These are the EAT in Never Let Monkeys Eat Bananas, and as such they are the fourth most common white blood cell type after Monocytes.

They make up roughly 1-4% of the white blood cells.
They pack a punch: inside each of them are many granuals or packets of cell killing poison.
One of their main roles is to go after large parasites like helminths and other parasitic worms.
