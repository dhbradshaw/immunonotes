# 5

Lymphocytes make up 20% to 40% of the white blood cells in the blood.
But what kinds of cells do lymphocytes include?

- T-cells (60-80%)
  - Helper (35-60%)
  - Cytotoxic (15-35%)
  - Regulatory (5-10%)
- B cells (10-20%)
- NK cells (5-15%)

I hadn't realized that T cells were several times more common than B cells.
