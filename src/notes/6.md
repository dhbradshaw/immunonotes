# 6

B cells can be broken down by stage. Most of them are Naive B cells.

| Activation State | Proportion of Blood B Cells | Primary Cell Types Included | Key Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unactivated / Resting** | **~80% - 95%** | Naïve B cells (~60-70%) & Antigen-experienced Memory B cells (~15-30%) | Circulating through blood and lymph nodes waiting for antigen encounter; resting metabolic state. |
| **Actively Activated / Responding** | **~1% - 5%** | Plasmablasts & Activated Germinal Center-derived B cells | Actively expanding and secreting antibodies during an ongoing pathogen threat. |
| **Immature / In Development** | **~3% - 5%** | Transitional B cells | Newly exported from bone marrow; undergoing final tolerance checks before becoming fully naïve. |
